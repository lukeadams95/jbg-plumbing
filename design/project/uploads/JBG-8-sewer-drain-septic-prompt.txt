Subheading: Four Signs You Might Have a Sewer Problem (4 numbered cards)
1. Your toilets won't flush or pipes don't drain. This is the most obvious indicator that something has gone terribly wrong and that you need a professional on the scene as quickly as possible. Wastewater that backflows can do serious property damage and cause health issues. You need this solved quickly.
2. Your yard becomes a bog. If your pets enter the house with muddy feet when it hasn't rained and you haven't run a sprinkler system — pay attention and investigate. Standing water is a good indicator that you may have broken sewer lines.
3. You smell a nasty odor on your property. Sewer lines don't smell very good and you might detect vapors on your property before you can see them.
4. Noticeably greener patches of the lawn during harsh summer months may mean the lawn is getting watered without your knowledge. A broken sewer line might "water" the lawn without your being aware. Your grass will love it, but you have a problem that JBG solves every day.

Subheading: Sewer Line Repair, Replacement, and Cleaning Services
Paragraph: We do quite a bit more than unclog piping and sewer lines. We offer an entire range of sewer services: including but not limited to main sewer line repair, full sewer line replacement, and sewer line cleaning. It is vital for homeowners to get the right help to repair or replace their sewer lines as soon as possible to avoid long-term damage. Our sewer line repair professionals are trained to address a number of sewer plumbing problems, including:
List:
- Bellied pipes. A section of a pipe or multiple pipes has sunk due to ground or soil conditions, creating a valley that collects debris and waste.
- Leaking joints. The seal that is used in between pipes may have decayed or broken down, which allows water to escape into the ground area around the pipes.
- Tree root interference. Tree roots or shrubbery may have dipped into the sewer line through cracks or pressure, which would cause clogs and prevent common sewer line cleaning.
- Off-grade piping. Established pipes, especially in older towns, may be constructed of degrading or outdated materials that have begun to corrode.
- Damaged pipes. Pipes can be cracked, punctured or caved in by moving soil, frozen ground, new home settling, and other factors.
- Sewer blockages. Grease and dirt buildup, as well as foreign objects, tend to restrict or entirely block proper water flow in a sewer line.

Subheading: Tree Roots and Sewer Lines
Paragraph 1: First, your sewer line can be made of cast iron, clay, or Orangeburg.
Paragraph 2: The thought of destroying your driveway or uprooting your lawn to repair your sewer line might unnerve you. We know. It's the last thing you want to consider. But if you have sewer line damage, there may not be a way around it.
Paragraph 3: A common cause of sewer line failure is thirsty tree roots. Sewer pipes and roots share the same space underground in both front and back yards, and over time they can become intertwined. Smaller roots searching out water sources will actually penetrate the line over time. You can consult with a tree doctor who can tell you if your particular type of tree is prone to deep roots (Post Oak) or stays above the foundation line (Live Oak) for example.
Paragraph 4: To access failed sewer lines, we must unearth the source and make repairs. Paragraph 5: Manually cutting the roots that are infringing upon your sewer lines is a short-term solution. Once cut, the roots will simply start over again, and multiply in fibers. A chemical or permanent uprooting process is a better long-term solution to a tree root and sewer line problem. We always hope to save root systems if possible and we defer to homeowners when decisions must be made. Give JBG a phone call when you encounter your next sewer line problem.

SECTION 4: SEPTIC TANK CLEANING (anchor id: septic-tank-cleaning)
Heading: Keep Your Septic System Running Smoothly with JBG Plumbing's Professional Cleaning Services
Subheading: Our septic cleaning services include: (5 numbered cards)
1. Septic Tank Pumping: We will remove the solid waste accumulated in your septic tank. This is a crucial step in maintaining the health of your septic system.
2. Septic Inspection: We will thoroughly inspect your septic tank and drain field to identify any potential issues that must be addressed. This includes checking for cracks, leaks, and clogs.
3. Repair and Maintenance: If we find any issues during the inspection, we will provide the necessary repair and maintenance services to keep your septic system functioning properly.
4. Drain Field Repair: If the drain field is not functioning correctly, we will repair or replace it to ensure your septic system can filter wastewater effectively.
5. Preventive Maintenance: Besides our cleaning services, we also offer preventative maintenance services to ensure your septic system stays in top condition. This includes regular inspections and pump-outs to keep your system running smoothly.

Subheading: Why Clean Your Septic System? Here are the Top 3 Reasons:
1. Prevent Costly Repairs: Regular septic tank cleaning helps prevent expensive repairs and replacements in the long run. Neglecting to clean your septic system can lead to clogs, backups, and even system failure.
2. Maintain a Healthy Environment: A poorly maintained septic system can pollute the environment, contaminating nearby water sources and posing a risk to public health. Keeping your septic system clean and well-maintained ensures a healthy environment for you and your community.
3. Save Money on Utilities: An overloaded septic system can cause your utility bills to skyrocket. By cleaning your septic system regularly, you can reduce the water and electricity you use, saving you money in the long run.

Callout box: According to the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality, it is recommended to clean septic systems every 3-5 years to maintain optimal performance and prevent potential health hazards. However, the pumping interval may vary depending on the size of the septic tank, the number of people using the system, and other factors.
